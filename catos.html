Description

CatOS is a collaborative 20-second long game developed by Kora Lee, Thao Tran & Adrian Frichitthavong. The team was formed as part of a game jam that utilised the art direction of Kora & Thao with the development and game design by Adrian.

In CatOS you must embark on a 20 second sprint to delete all of the neighbours cat's pitcures before your own cat discovers what you've been storing on your computer. To do so you must weave your way through every advertisement, every obsolete Teams meeting call and make sure all the evidence of other cats are deleted.
