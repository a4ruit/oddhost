Description

Outdoor forest installation on dual vertical displays. Real-time hand tracking drives an ASCII boid flock as a live force field.

Distance between two hands controls the frequency, speed, count and scale of the flock. An ASCII whale hunts it, while world-space HUDs follow the audience's hands.
